# Decision Records Contract

Two-tier decision system.

## Blueprint and Charter decisions

Routine decisions about scope, terminology, stage sequencing, ordinary defaults,
and artifact layout may be incorporated when the user approves the governing
artifact as a whole.

## Formal `DEC-###` records

Required when a decision is:

- Foundational
- Disputed
- Expensive to reverse
- Security-sensitive
- Likely to be revisited
- Superseding an earlier authority
- Materially changing the approved research graph

Formal decisions require **explicit human approval**.
