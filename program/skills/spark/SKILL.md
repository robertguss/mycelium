# Spark

First sitting inside a new instance.

## Steps

1. Read `index.md` and `mycelium.toml` (not the whole tree).
2. Interview the human **one question at a time**. Recommend each time.
   Stop when the first thought is stateable. Do not invent scope, tracks,
   or conclusions.
3. `mycelium state exploring`
4. `mycelium new question "…"` or `mycelium new decision "…"` for that
   thought.
5. `mycelium check`

Done: `state` is `exploring`, one artifact exists, `check` is green.

Do not start a blueprint or a research graph. Do not invent a hub.
Do not `git commit` unless the human asks.
