# Chief Architect Synthesis Contract

## Purpose

Convert research into **one** proposed definitive specification. Synthesis is
**decision-making**, not summarization.

## Required behavior

- Read all materially relevant reports completely.
- Disposition every substantive `REC-###`.
- Resolve contradictory recommendations.
- Preserve locked decisions.
- Reject weak or inapplicable machinery.
- Normalize terminology.
- Define one coherent architecture or solution.
- Convert decisions into `REQ-###` requirements.
- Define risks and open questions.
- Define first implementation strategy and phase boundaries at high level.
- Leave no foundational decision to the implementer unless a bounded spike is
  explicitly required.

## Recommendation dispositions

Every inherited recommendation receives exactly one:

- Accepted
- Accepted with modification
- Merged
- Deferred
- Rejected
- Superseded
- Not applicable

## Proposed specification status

Normally: `Proposed — pending adversarial review`.
